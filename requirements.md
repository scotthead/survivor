# Survivor Last Man Standing

I want to build a website that helps coordindate a game for me. The goal of the game is to be the one who picks the winner on the TV show Survivor. The game works like this:

1. Pick a player on the current season after the first episode
2. If your player gets eliminated you are out of the game
3. If your player is the last player standing while all others are eliminated, then you win.

You don't have to pick the actual winner of the whole series, you just have to be the last character standing.

I want an experience that lets players login to the site, either setup a group or join a group, look through the profiles of each player, pick a player (the same player can't be picked twice), and then track along with the season to see if their player has been eliminated and who else still has a player in the game. They should know if they are the last player standing and that they have won the game.

Users should also be able to see past games and how well they did.

When browsing the contestants, users should be able to see their names, their pictures, their jobs, where they are from, where they live now and some details about them.
